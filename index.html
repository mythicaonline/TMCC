Community Guidelines

Server Rules

1. No Griefing or Stealing within claims.

Not claimed areas are fair game!

2. Don't be rude or harass other players.

This is a community. It's a place where people come to relax and have a good time with others. Any toxic, derogatory or racist language will not be tolerated.

3. No Cheating!

Hacks, exploits, glitches and unfair modifications are prohibited.

4. No Advertising!

TMCC does not allow any kind of server advertising of other MC servers or services.

5. No 18+ inappropriate or NSFW Content/skins/nickanames etc.

Do NOT post or build any inappropriate / NSFW (not safe for work) content in any way, shape or form. This rule is to ensure that the players do not come in contact with inappropriate content. Do not engage in "NSFW" or sexual discussion, nor topics related to self-harm, drugs, politics, or other controversial matters.

6. No excessive swearing.

This server allows swearing, just not too much of it. If you want to swear, keep in mind rule 2.

7. Punishment evasions

Trying to evade a punishment or bypass a punishment is NOT allowed. Once the punishment has been given, the player in question must wait until this punishment is over. The punishment will be extended or upscaled if needed. It will also be given to any alts.

8. Don't promote drugs or drug use

This includes but not limited to:
Talking or discussing drugs, drug use or other drug related subjects.
Renaming items to resemble IRL drugs.
Pretending to sell drugs with in-game items (named or unnamed).

9. Use common sense.

Not every situation can be covered by a rule.
Staff reserve the right to take action against behavior that seriously harms the server or its community.
Punishments may range from warnings to temporary or permanent bans depending on the situation.
